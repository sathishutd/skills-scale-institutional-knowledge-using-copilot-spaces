# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Quality Assurance / Test Engineers

### Role Summary
Quality Assurance (QA) and Test Engineers validate that features meet acceptance criteria, work reliably in real-world scenarios, and do not regress existing behavior.

### Responsibilities
- Define and execute test plans for new features and changes
- Validate acceptance criteria and release readiness
- Identify defects, regressions, and usability issues
- Collaborate with developers to reproduce and triage bugs
- Provide evidence for deployment and rollout confidence

### Goals
- Improve product quality and confidence before release
- Reduce production defects and support escalations
- Balance speed of delivery with reliability and user trust

### Typical Communication
- Test case reviews and bug triage sessions
- Release readiness checks and QA sign-off meetings
- Collaboration with Product and Engineering on risk and quality trade-offs

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works closely with Developers to confirm fixes and regression coverage
- Partners with Product Managers to validate business scenarios and acceptance criteria
- Keeps Project Managers informed of risks, defects, and delivery readiness

---

## UX Designers and Researchers

### Role Summary
UX Designers and Researchers ensure product experiences are usable, accessible, and aligned to user needs. They translate customer problems into design decisions and test hypotheses with evidence.

### Responsibilities
- Define user flows, interaction patterns, and accessibility standards
- Gather feedback through user interviews, usability testing, and analytics
- Partner with Product on problem framing and opportunity discovery
- Validate that proposed solutions are intuitive and aligned with customer expectations

### Goals
- Improve usability, clarity, and customer satisfaction
- Reduce friction in critical user journeys
- Align product decisions with observed user needs and business outcomes

### Typical Communication
- Design reviews and user research summaries
- Feedback loops with Product and Engineering on prototypes and implementation details
- Stakeholder briefings for design direction and trade-offs

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Collaborates with Product Managers to define user problems and success criteria
- Works with Developers to ensure implementation matches the intended experience
- Supports Project Managers by helping sequence design, validation, and rollout activities

---

## Engineering Managers / Tech Leads

### Role Summary
Engineering Managers and Tech Leads guide delivery quality, team health, and technical execution. They ensure the engineering organization can deliver effectively while staying aligned with project goals.

### Responsibilities
- Coordinate technical planning, dependencies, and resourcing across teams
- Guide architecture decisions, implementation standards, and technical trade-offs
- Support developer productivity, delivery health, and quality practices
- Partner with Product and Project leadership on sequencing and risk management

### Goals
- Deliver dependable engineering execution
- Maintain sustainable team velocity and technical quality
- Reduce avoidable delays, blockers, and rework

### Typical Communication
- Team status reviews and dependency management meetings
- Architecture and technical decision discussions
- Escalation updates with PMs and senior stakeholders

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Product Managers on scope, priorities, and technical feasibility
- Coordinates with Project Managers on timing, dependencies, and risk mitigation
- Supports Developers by clarifying technical direction and delivery constraints

---

## Security and Compliance Leads

### Role Summary
Security and Compliance Leads ensure that projects meet organizational standards for privacy, data protection, and secure implementation. They reduce risk and ensure delivery is safe for customers and the business.

### Responsibilities
- Review architecture, data flows, and features for security and compliance risks
- Define required controls, approval checkpoints, and policy alignment
- Partner with engineering teams to remediate vulnerabilities and review findings
- Validate readiness for deployment in regulated or sensitive environments

### Goals
- Reduce security and compliance exposure
- Protect customer trust and organizational risk posture
- Enable delivery without violating agreed controls

### Typical Communication
- Security reviews and policy alignment meetings
- Risk assessments and control validation checkpoints
- Escalation updates for high-risk or time-sensitive issues

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Developers and Engineering Managers to design secure implementations
- Provides feedback to Product Managers and Project Managers on risk, timing, and approval gates
- Aligns with QA to validate security-related acceptance criteria and regression protection

---

## Customer Success / Support Leads

### Role Summary
Customer Success and Support Leads represent the voice of the customer after launch and during adoption. They help teams understand customer pain points, supportability needs, and post-release quality issues.

### Responsibilities
- Monitor customer feedback, support trends, and adoption barriers
- Surface improvement opportunities based on real usage and incidents
- Partner with Product and Engineering on resolution prioritization
- Ensure support documentation, onboarding, and escalation paths are clear

### Goals
- Improve customer health and retention
- Reduce friction in the support experience
- Turn customer feedback into product and process improvements

### Typical Communication
- Support and customer feedback reviews
- Launch readiness and post-release feedback loops
- Escalation meetings with Product and Project leadership

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Provides Product Managers with evidence about customer pain points and unmet needs
- Shares operational insights with Project Managers to support rollout, readiness, and follow-up actions
- Helps Developers understand real-world defects and supportability concerns

---

## Executive Sponsors / Stakeholders

### Role Summary
Executive Sponsors and Stakeholders provide strategic direction, funding, and decision support. They ensure projects remain aligned with business goals and organizational priorities.

### Responsibilities
- Confirm business value, funding, and strategic priority
- Review milestones, risks, and major decisions
- Support prioritization trade-offs and cross-functional alignment
- Align project outcomes with broader organizational goals

### Goals
- Maximize strategic impact and return on investment
- Maintain executive visibility into progress and risk
- Support timely decisions that unblock delivery

### Typical Communication
- Steering committee updates and milestone reviews
- Decision forums for trade-offs and escalations
- Briefings on value delivery and stakeholder concerns

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Receives status updates from Project Managers and Product Managers
- Provides direction to Product and Engineering leaders on strategic trade-offs
- Helps resolve escalations that require business or portfolio-level decisions

---

## How these personas are used in the exercise
- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- A project team often includes several of these roles at the same time, and effective collaboration depends on clear ownership, communication, and shared decision-making.
